## What Makes It Work

The grinder is the useful part of the puzzle. Its catch cup stays with the beans and doubles as my scoop, while the grinder body nests almost perfectly inside the AeroPress.

So the grinder is not just another cylinder to pack. Most of it goes into space that would otherwise be awkward or unused.

## Packing Sequence

![Animated packing sequence showing the coffee and tea kit being packed into the striped pouch.](/images/hobbies/1l-coffee-tea-kit/packing-sequence.gif)

The packing order is:

1. Keep the grinder catch cup with the beans.
2. Put the grinder body inside the AeroPress.
3. Bundle the heater, tea, infuser, and grinder handle.
4. Let the soft parts fill the gaps.

![Blue-and-white striped Daiso pouch closed after the complete kit is packed.](/images/hobbies/1l-coffee-tea-kit/packed-bag.jpeg)

I am calling the pouch "~1 L" rather than claiming an exact rated capacity. It is an older tapered Daiso insulated lunch bag. My measurements are about 14 x 8 cm at the base and about 14 cm tall, but it narrows toward the zipper and rounds out when packed.

One practical note on the heater: check voltage and power compatibility for the destination, and follow its instructions for minimum immersion depth and container suitability.
